title: Low Tide
category: historias-poesia
---

The sea has stepped back from the harbor wall\\
and left its furniture on the sand:\\
a shoe, a rope, the ribs of a boat,\\
everything the water was keeping for itself.

Gulls walk the wet ground like inspectors.\\
The mud holds each footprint a moment,\\
then lets it go, a little at a time,\\
as though forgiveness were a matter of patience.

By evening the water comes back without announcing it.\\
First the shoe is gone, then the rope.\\
You stay on the wall anyway,\\
learning the sound of something returning.
